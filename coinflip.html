The rules

Choose heads or tails, set a bet, and flip. The coin is a true 50/50.

Win: you get 2× your bet back — your original stake plus the same amount in profit.

Lose: the bet is gone.

There is no house edge on this one. It is pure double-or-nothing. Your streak resets after a loss.

Streak hunting

Win five flips in a row to unlock On Fire. Your best streak is saved across sessions.

Play money only. No real gambling.

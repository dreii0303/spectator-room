Scoring

Hits: 6 points each.

Accuracy bonus: up to 100 points for clicking the right holes.

Combo bonus: 2 points per hit in your best uninterrupted streak.

A focused run can pay 250+ points. Panic-clicking pays much less.

How to play

Press Start Run. A mole pops out of one of the nine holes. Click it before it disappears.

Missing a mole or clicking an empty hole resets your combo. The run lasts 30 seconds.

Scoring

Base: 50 points for clearing the board.

Time bonus: up to 200 points. Every second costs you 5.

Move bonus: up to 100 points for solving it in close to the minimum 8 moves.

Finish in 25 seconds or less to unlock the Memory Ace achievement.

How to play

Flip two cards at a time. Matching symbols stay face up. Non-matches flip back after a moment.

The timer starts on your first flip and stops when the last pair is matched.

Play money only. No real gambling.

      How to play

      Pick your bet (minimum 10 points), then spin. Three matching symbols pay the big multipliers. Exactly two matching symbols pay the smaller ones.

      Every bet puts 4% into the jackpot. Three 7s wins the entire pool and resets it to 1,000.

      Symbols are weighted — 7s are rare, cherries are common. The math is tuned to a house edge so the arcade stays balanced. Play money only.

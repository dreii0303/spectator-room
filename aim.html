Scoring

Hits: 4 points each.

Accuracy bonus: up to 100 points for hitting what you aim at.

Speed bonus: up to 150 points for fast average reactions.

A clean, quick run can pay 300+ points. A panic-spam run pays a lot less.

How to play

Press Start Run and click the purple targets for 30 seconds. Each hit spawns a new target somewhere else.

Targets you ignore will disappear and respawn. Clicking empty space still counts as a shot, so aim deliberately.

Play money only. No real gambling.

Your bet is locked in when you launch. Cash out at any time after the first 0.25 seconds. The house edge is built into the crash curve.

How it works

Set a bet and launch. The multiplier starts at 1.00× and climbs in real time.

Hit cash out and your payout is bet × current multiplier. Wait too long and the round crashes, taking the bet with it.

The longer you wait, the bigger the payout — and the bigger the chance you lose everything. That is the whole game.

The math

The crash point is random, with a 3% house edge. There is a 3% chance of an instant crash, and the odds get thinner as the multiplier climbs.

Your best cashout multiplier is saved in your player file.

How the dice pay

Two fair six-sided dice are rolled. The total is between 2 and 12.

Under 7 wins on 2–6 and pays 2.3×. Over 7 wins on 8–12 and pays 2.3×.

Exactly 7 wins on a seven and pays 5.8×. The other two calls lose.

Why the payouts differ

There are 36 possible rolls. Fifteen are under seven, fifteen are over seven, and six are exactly seven. The smaller chance pays more.

The payouts are tuned to a small house edge so the shared balance stays healthy. Every point is play money.

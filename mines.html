How Mines works

Every round hides five mines in a 5×5 grid. Reveal a safe tile to collect a gem and raise your cash-out multiplier.

You can cash out after the first gem. The payout is your bet multiplied by the current multiplier. Hit a mine and the bet is gone.

Multipliers

The multiplier is based on the real odds of surviving that many picks, with a small house edge. More gems means a much bigger cash-out — and a much smaller chance of getting there.

Find all 20 gems and the round cashes out automatically at the maximum multiplier.

Table rules

Goal: beat the dealer's hand without going over 21. Number cards are their value, face cards are 10, and aces count as 1 or 11.

Hit takes another card. Stand ends your turn. Double doubles your bet, deals one final card, and stands automatically.

Blackjack is an ace plus a ten-value card on the first two cards. It pays 3:2.

A push (equal totals) returns your bet. The dealer stands on all 17s.

Good to know

You can only double on your first two cards, and only if your balance covers the extra bet.

The shoe is shuffled automatically when it runs low.

Play money only. No real gambling.
